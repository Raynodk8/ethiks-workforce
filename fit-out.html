Questions

Straight answers.

How much does a temporary site manager cost?

You pay one rate, hourly or daily, agreed before the manager starts. It covers their pay, tax, National Insurance, holiday pay and our service. There are no other fees.

Do you fund the wages?

Yes. We pay your site manager every week. You settle on the 5th of each month for the month before, so the weekly wages do not come out of your cash flow. This runs while your account is within its agreed limit and paid up to date.

What happens if we go over our limit or pay late?

We pause the supply until your account is back within its limit and up to date. We tell you before that happens. Your manager is still paid for the work they have already done.

Is there a fee if we take the manager on ourselves?

No. Ethiks charges no transfer fee. If the manager was your own person and we were funding their pay, there is nothing to pay anyone. If they came through the UrProject marketplace, UrProject's standard charge of £50 a week applies for as long as they work for you, with no margin on their hours. UrProject is our sister company and has its own monthly plan.

What checks do you do?

Identity, right to work in the UK, and the tickets you ask for, such as SMSTS, CSCS and first aid, before the manager starts.

Can I hire direct instead?

Yes. Our sister company UrProject lets you hire and manage site people direct. Ethiks Workforce is for when you want the manager found, paid and invoiced for you.
