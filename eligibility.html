A few details about your company and your expected monthly spend. We check your eligibility and cover limit, and email you the answer.

How the check works

  • You send your company details and your expected monthly spend.
  • We check your eligibility and cover limit.
  • We email you the credit available and your cover limit. You decide from there.
  • No need to call or discuss anything until you have the answer, unless you want to.
  • There is no obligation, and nothing to pay for the check.

What the cover limit means

The limit is the most that can be owed to us at any time for the managers we supply. You pay us 30 days from the end of the month.

For UK limited companies and limited liability partnerships. We share the details you give us with our finance provider and our credit insurer, who may check them with credit reference agencies. See our privacy notice.
