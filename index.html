Questions

How much does a temporary site manager cost?

You pay one day rate, agreed before the manager starts. It covers their pay, tax, National Insurance, holiday pay and our service. There are no other fees.

Do you fund the wages?

Yes. We pay your site manager every week. You settle on the 5th of each month for the month before, so the weekly wages do not come out of your cash flow.

What checks do you do?

Identity, right to work in the UK, and the tickets you ask for, such as SMSTS, CSCS and first aid, before the manager starts.

Can I hire direct instead?

Yes. Our sister company UrProject lets you hire and manage site people direct. Ethiks Workforce is for when you want the manager found, paid and invoiced for you.
