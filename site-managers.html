Site management work, paid every week

Ethiks Workforce places site managers, project managers, site supervisors and quantity surveyors on fit-out and construction projects across the UK.

How it works for you

  • Tell us your role, the areas you cover and when you are free.
  • We check your identity, right to work and tickets once, and keep them on record.
  • We put you forward for projects that fit, and agree the day rate with you before you start.
  • You are paid every week through our payroll partner, whether or not the client has paid us.
  • You record your time on the UrProject app and the client approves it each week.

What you need

  • Experience running sites or projects in fit-out or construction
  • SMSTS, or SSSTS for supervisors
  • A current CSCS card
  • First aid at work
  • The right to work in the UK

Ethiks Workforce acts as an employment business. Before any assignment you get a Key Information Document showing how you will be paid and what is deducted.

We use your details to find you work and to carry out the checks the law requires. See our privacy notice.
