How the check works

  • You send your company details and the limit you need.
  • We check your company with our finance provider.
  • We email you whether we can supply you, and the limit we can offer.
  • There is no obligation, and nothing to pay for the check.

What the limit means

The limit is the most that can be owed to us at any time for the managers we supply. You settle on the 5th of each month for the month before, so a rough guide is the cost of your managers for about five weeks.

For UK limited companies and limited liability partnerships. We share the details you give us with our finance provider and our credit insurer, who may check them with credit reference agencies. See our privacy notice.
