Privacy notice

How Ethiks Workforce uses personal information.

Who we are

Ethiks Workforce is a trading division of Ethiks Site Services Ltd, company number 10755925, registered office 167-169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London, England, W1W 5PF. Ethiks Site Services Ltd is the controller of the personal information described here.

If you are a client

When you ask us for a site manager we collect your company name and number, and the name, phone number and email address of the person we deal with.

We use this to answer your request, to check whether we can supply your company, to agree terms and to send invoices. Before we quote, we share your company details with our finance provider and our credit insurer, who may check them with credit reference agencies. We supply limited companies and limited liability partnerships only.

If you are a site manager or other candidate

We collect your name, contact details, work history, qualifications and tickets, proof of identity and proof of your right to work in the UK, and the times you work on an assignment.

We use this to find you work, to carry out the checks that the law requires of an employment business, to put you forward to clients for roles you have agreed to be considered for, and to arrange your pay.

We share it with the client for a role you are put forward for, with the payroll company that employs and pays you, and with UrProject Ltd, our sister company, whose platform holds your verified documents and timesheets.

Our lawful reasons

We rely on our legitimate interests in running a staffing business, on the steps needed to enter into and carry out a contract with you, and on our legal duties, including right to work checks and the record keeping rules for employment businesses.

How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as we are working with you and for at least one year after we last provided services to you, as the rules for employment businesses require. Tax and payroll records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you about our services. Email privacy@ethiks.co.uk or write to us at the registered office above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
